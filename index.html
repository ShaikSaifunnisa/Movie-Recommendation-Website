Content-based

Match by genre

Compares the genre profile of a film you enjoyed against every other title using cosine similarity, and returns the closest matches.

Collaborative

Match by viewer

Finds viewers whose past ratings look most like yours, then recommends what they rated highly that you haven't seen yet.
